SETTING

Okovye: a country living on the body of a god

Dark Slavic fantasy with no orcs and no elves: the nav, the Oathbound, rumor-mongering taverns and the Gilded Decree. By day Okovye trades, litigates and weaves politics; by night the world belongs to the Mgla. The player chooses the hour of leaving camp — and pays for the choice.

✦ 5 regions — from the Polesie marshes to the drowned Still Sea
✦ The Mgla — the world’s tension meter: gold dug from the god’s body brings the eternal night closer
