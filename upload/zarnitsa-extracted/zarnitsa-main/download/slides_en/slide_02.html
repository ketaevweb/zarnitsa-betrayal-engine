LOGLINE

The god is dead. Night is coming. The Vataga remembers.

Thirty years ago, people killed the last god — Zlatoglav. Since then the Mgla rises from the earth each night, and the country grows rich digging gold out of his body. Every ingot unearthed brings the eternal night closer. The player is the Oathbound Ataman, leading a vataga of sworn brothers: six companions with their own memory, hidden agendas and a very real capacity for betrayal.
