GAMEPLAY LOOP

Contract → Camp → Night

1
CONTRACT

A job board with bargaining: price, deadlines, currency, witnesses. Terms shift in dialogue — witnesses remember the deal, and a stingy haggle comes back through a companion’s memory journal.

→
2
CAMP

One conversation before nightfall: the raid party — up to three brothers; gear: oils, traps, strings. Gifts and oaths stay in the vataga’s memory.

→
3
NIGHT

A real-time raid into the Mgla under the Oath Pause. After the fight — the Right of Speech: a brother may step out of the shadow with a grievance, and the ataman answers.

Between nights the world lives without the player: factions make their moves, rumors spread through taverns, the Mgla gathers strength.
