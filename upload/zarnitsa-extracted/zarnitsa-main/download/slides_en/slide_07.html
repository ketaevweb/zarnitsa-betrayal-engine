Real time under the Oath Pause

The spacebar freezes time at any moment: the ataman issues orders to the brothers, picks a Zaritsa form and resumes the fight. Reflex matters less than half an hour of preparation — “run in and swing” tactics are punished naturally.

1 · White — five empowered strikes in a row

4 · Red — detonates the accumulated Mgla across the causeway
Heat and overload: every form heats the power — in overload, strikes burn your own. Oathfire holds the line: the nav skirt the flame in a wide arc.
